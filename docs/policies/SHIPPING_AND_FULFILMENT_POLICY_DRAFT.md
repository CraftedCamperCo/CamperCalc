# Shipping and Fulfilment Policy (Draft)

Business: Crafted Camper Co (Yorkshire) LTD  
Product: CamperPlan by Crafted (physical order fulfilment)  
Version: Draft v1 (2026-03-09)

> This draft is adapted for CamperPlan launch and references practical structure used by batteriesandsolar.co.uk delivery policy.  
> Validate courier SLAs and surcharges before publishing.

## 1) Fulfilment Model

Orders are fulfilled via a mix of:

- in-house processing, and/or
- approved dropship supplier partners.

Availability and lead times may vary by product and supplier.

## 2) Shipping Methods (UK)

Typical methods:

- Standard Parcel (small/medium items)
- Large Parcel (heavy items)
- Pallet / Freight (oversized or hazardous goods)
- Click & Collect (if enabled)

## 3) Dispatch and Delivery Times

- Target dispatch window: 1-2 business days for in-stock items.
- Delivery window:
  - Standard parcel: 1-3 business days after dispatch.
  - Large parcel/freight: 2-5+ business days after dispatch.
- Cut-off times and weekend/holiday schedules apply.

## 4) Surcharges and Exceptions

Additional charges may apply for:

- dangerous goods,
- oversized/long-length goods,
- remote locations and special handling,
- non-mainland UK destinations.

Any surcharge is shown at checkout where possible.

## 5) Tracking

- Tracking is provided where courier supports it.
- Some freight services may provide limited milestone tracking.

## 6) Delivery Requirements

- Signature may be required for high-value or specialist items.
- We do not guarantee safe-place delivery for restricted product classes.
- Customer is responsible for accurate delivery details at checkout.

## 7) Damaged, Missing, or Lost Deliveries

If goods are damaged, missing, or not received:

1. Contact support as soon as possible.
2. Provide order number and photo evidence (item + packaging).
3. We open investigation with courier/supplier.

Target investigation timeline: 7-10 business days (varies by carrier).

## 8) Fulfilment Delays

Delays may occur due to:

- supplier stock changes,
- courier disruption,
- severe weather,
- customs/regulatory checks.

Where delays are known, we will notify by email.

## 9) Order Confirmation and Fulfilment Emails

Customer receives:

- order confirmation after successful payment,
- dispatch update (where available),
- fulfilment/tracking update when supported.
