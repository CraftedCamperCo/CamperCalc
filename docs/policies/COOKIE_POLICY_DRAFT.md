## 1) What this policy covers

This policy explains how CamperPlan websites and related services use cookies and similar technologies.

## 2) What cookies are

Cookies are small text files saved on your device when you visit a website. They help features work, remember preferences, and support analytics.

## 3) Cookie categories we use

### A) Strictly Necessary

Required for core site functions (security, session continuity, basket state, checkout flow).  
These do not require consent under UK guidance when used only for essential service operation.

### B) Analytics

Used to understand site/app usage and improve performance (for example, Google Analytics events).  
Enabled only with consent where required.

### C) Functional Preferences

Used to remember language, display, and usability preferences.

### D) Marketing / Advertising

If enabled in future, these require explicit consent and clear controls.

## 4) Third-party services

We may use third-party providers (for example analytics, payment, embedded content).  
Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies.

## 5) Managing consent

Users can:

- accept/reject non-essential cookies,
- update preferences at any time,
- clear cookies via browser settings.

## 6) Retention

Cookie retention varies by category and provider.  
A cookie table should be added before launch with exact names and durations.

## 7) Your rights

You have rights over personal data linked to cookies under UK GDPR, including access, deletion, and objection where applicable.
