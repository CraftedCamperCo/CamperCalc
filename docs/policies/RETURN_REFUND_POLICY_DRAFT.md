# Return and Refund Policy (Draft)

Business: Crafted Camper Co (Yorkshire) LTD  
Product: CamperPlan by Crafted (app + related products)  
Version: Draft v1 (2026-03-09)

> This draft is adapted for CamperPlan launch based on UK e-commerce norms and the policy structure used by batteriesandsolar.co.uk.  
> Get legal review before public release.

## 1) Scope

This policy applies to products purchased through CamperPlan and related online sales channels operated by Crafted Camper Co (Yorkshire) LTD.

- Physical goods: components, kits, accessories.
- Digital goods/services: schematics, guides, premium digital access.

## 2) Change-of-Mind Returns (Physical Goods)

- Customers have **14 days** from delivery to notify us of cancellation/return.
- Customers then have a further **14 days** to return goods (total 28 days from delivery).
- Returned goods must be:
  - unused,
  - in original condition,
  - in original packaging,
  - with proof of purchase.

## 3) Return Shipping Costs

- Customer pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns.
- Original outbound shipping is non-refundable except where required by law.
- If goods are faulty, damaged, or not as described, we will reimburse reasonable return postage.

## 4) Faulty / Damaged / Not-as-Described Items

- Report within **1 business day** for shipping damage where possible.
- Include order number, photos of item and packaging, and issue description.
- We may offer troubleshooting first to avoid unnecessary returns.
- If fault confirmed and in warranty:
  - replacement, repair, or refund offered in line with UK consumer law.

## 5) Inspection and Refund Timing

- Returned items are inspected on receipt.
- Approved refunds are sent to original payment method.
- Target processing time: **within 5 business days** after approval.

## 6) Partial Refunds / Deductions

Partial refunds may apply where goods:

- are returned outside policy windows,
- are not in original condition,
- are missing parts not caused by our error.

## 7) Non-Returnable Items (unless faulty or required by law)

- Custom or made-to-order items.
- Opened consumables/adhesives where resale is not possible.
- Digital products once delivered/accessed (e.g., downloadable schematics, video access), except where legally required.

## 8) Sale/Promotional Items

- Sale items are refundable at the amount paid at time of purchase.
- No cash equivalent above amount paid.

## 9) Exchange Policy

- Exchanges are offered where stock is available.
- If replacement is unavailable, a refund or suitable alternative may be offered.

## 10) How to Start a Return

Provide:

- Order number
- Name and email used at checkout
- Item(s) and reason for return
- Photo evidence where faulty/damaged

This policy does not limit your statutory rights under applicable UK consumer law.
